The Yoo Jae Ha Music Concours Alumni Association is an independent organization separate from the Yoo Jae Ha Music Scholarship Foundation (yjh.or.kr). The Foundation hosts the Yoo Jae Ha Music Concours, while the Alumni Association organizes it. This website, yjhdongmoon.or.kr, is the official website of the Alumni Association.
